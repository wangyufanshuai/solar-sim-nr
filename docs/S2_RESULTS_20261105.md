## What this means

The FO-CCZ4 RK4 kernel evolves a weak periodic wave correctly with the designed FD2, FD4 and
RK4 convergence orders. Its response to grid-scale noise is not yet shown to be stable; the
next step is a diagnostic long run to tell bounded, linear and exponential growth apart,
followed by a new registration.

- No field grows exponentially over 40 crossing times.
- The lapse grows linearly. Its spatial mean drifts at d<ln α>/dt = −3.265e-12, matching
