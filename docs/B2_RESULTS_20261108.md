Gate: both orders ≥ 2.6 and E49 ≤ 1e-2. The magnitude passes; the orders do not — they fall
towards 2 instead of the ≈ 3 expected for SBP(4,2) with energy-stable SAT.

Where the error is (from the B2-c diagnostic on the same sine problem, Nx = 17): the largest
errors are in Ã and D at the face node and its neighbours, from the first step on; the
interior error is much smaller. **Hypothesis, not yet tested:** the "+1 order" gain of SBP
boundary closures relies on boundary truncation errors being carried out of the domain;
FO-CCZ4 has many zero-speed characteristic fields (the auxiliary constraints D − ½∂h etc.)
whose second-order boundary truncation errors stay where they are made, which would give
global order 2. A per-characteristic-field error split at the face would test this — done
below: the hypothesis is rejected.

## What this means

The characteristic SAT boundaries are consistent (errors converge, the long run stays bounded
for at least 2.2 crossing times) but not yet at the accuracy the gates asked for: the observed
order is about 2, not 3, and the pulse test leaves a residual of 0.25 A at Nx = 33. B2 is not
passed. The next steps are a per-characteristic error split at the faces (tests the zero-speed
hypothesis) and then either constraint-preserving boundary conditions or a corrected
boundary treatment for the zero-speed fields — each with a new registration.
